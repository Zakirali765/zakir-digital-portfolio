Privacy, in plain language.

This page describes how this portfolio is implemented. It is not a legal certification. Updated 10 October 2026.

What this website does

This is a static portfolio with no accounts, database, analytics, advertising trackers or embedded external chatbot. Fonts and images are served locally. The support assistant example uses prewritten answers and has no free-text field.

Your project brief

The enquiry composer processes your name, business name, email, town or service area, website URL, service interest and message in your browser. The site does not submit this form to a server, store it in localStorage or send it to ZAKIR DIGITAL automatically. Please avoid sensitive information.

You can copy the brief to your device’s clipboard or download it as a text file. These actions happen only when you choose them. Your browser may restore form values according to its own settings.

Email and WhatsApp drafts

Zakir will use the details you choose to send only to reply to your enquiry. This page has no direct “Send enquiry” integration.

“Open email draft” passes your brief to your chosen email application using a mailto link. “Open WhatsApp draft” opens WhatsApp with the brief included in the destination URL. That step shares the message content with the application you open. Opening a draft does not send a message; you review and send it yourself.

WhatsApp and your email provider have their own privacy practices. Direct contact links also open external services. Information you choose to send there is handled by those providers and received by Zakir through the chosen channel.

Fictional project demos

The three sample websites are concepts for fictional businesses. Their enquiry examples show local messages only. They do not collect personal details, submit forms or take bookings.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This site does not add analytics or advertising trackers. Learn more inGitHub’s privacy statement.
