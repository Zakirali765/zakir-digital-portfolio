07 / FAQ

A few things
you might be wondering.

Are you based in the UK?

No. I’m based in Pakistan and work remotely with UK businesses. I’ll be clear about response times and will aim to reply during UK working hours where possible.

How much does a website cost?

Small-business websites typically start from £250–£499 depending on scope. I’ll confirm a clear quote before any work starts. Chatbot setup and recurring platform costs are separate.

How long does it take?

Typically 4–7 days after I receive the upfront payment, your content and any required access, depending on scope. If I need something from you, I’ll tell you what is missing.

How many changes can I request?

Two revision rounds are included within the agreed scope. I’ll explain what counts as a revision before we start. Additional work is agreed separately before proceeding.

Who owns the website?

You receive ownership of the agreed website deliverables after full payment, subject to third-party licences. The remaining 50% is paid after approval, before handover and the agreed launch.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, if you have one, text about your services, approved photos, service areas, opening hours and contact details. For a redesign, I’ll also need the agreed access to your current website, domain or hosting.

What can the optional assistant do?

It can answer common questions using verified information about your business. It does not collect personal data, generate leads, provide quotations or make bookings.

Do you guarantee more leads, sales or Google rankings?

No. A clearer website can help visitors understand and contact your business, but I do not guarantee leads, revenue, conversions or rankings.

Can I keep my domain and hosting?

Your existing domain and suitable hosting can usually be retained. Any domain, hosting, maintenance or optional platform costs are discussed and agreed separately.
