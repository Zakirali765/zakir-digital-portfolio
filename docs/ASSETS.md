- Space Grotesk: display/headings, @fontsource-variable/space-grotesk 5.3.0. Official source: https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- DM Sans: body/controls, @fontsource-variable/dm-sans 5.3.0. Official source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
